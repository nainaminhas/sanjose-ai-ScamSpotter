# Who Handles This?: AI for finding the right government agency

Client: City of San José, Resident Services and Language Access
Team: [Your name, teammate names], Aug–Oct 2026
My part: I built the prototype in Replit, wrote resident interview writeups, and designed the final pitch deck.

## The problem
San José residents who get a government notice often can't tell if the City, County, or State handles it. They call the wrong office, get transferred, and risk missing deadlines, which hits hardest for the 58.9% of residents who speak a language other than English at home.

## What we built
- A web app where residents describe a letter or issue in everyday words (English, Spanish, Vietnamese, Chinese, Tagalog, or German) and get matched to the agency that handles it.
- The user sees the agency name, a link to its official page, and one clear next step.
- When the match is unclear, sources conflict, or the issue could be urgent, the app flags it for staff review so a person makes the final call.

## What I would do next
Red-teaming showed a gas leak report returned "staff review needed" with no emergency guidance, and no real staff actually monitor flagged cases. I'd add an emergency check that points to 911 or PG&E first, and learn how to connect the app to a live 311 queue so flagged cases reach a real person.
